## Accepted

- `deadman check 003` reports 0 errors and 0 warnings (9,367 words, about 62 minutes).
- Disclosure: the host's first and last lines both say the guest is an AI recreation, and the mid-show reminder (line 489) is fine.
- Line 127 puts "excelled in making verses" in quotes. These are Newton's own reported words (to Conduit), quoted in brewster-0005 and chittenden-0003, not a biographer's sentence.
- Line 181 uses "in the year 1666, or before." It matches chittenden-0005 verbatim (Newton's own statement).
- Lines 205, 585 and 589 react to posthumous facts (the chair, the monument, the couplet) that the host just explained. This is allowed. The citations point to the source of the host's information.
- Line 253, "May 1687". This is what the cited source says (brewster-0124). Modern accounts usually date publication to early July 1687 (Halley's letter of 5 July 1687). Simply saying "in 1687" would be safe either way. This is optional and does not block recording.
- Line 323 ("In a later edition of the Principia"). The General Scholium was added in 1713, within Newton's lifetime, so this is not an anachronism.
- Line 385 (Diamond). Newton neither confirms nor invents. He keeps to brewster-0177 (Newton "never refers to the experiments" lost) and brewster-0183 (papers burned by a candle while he was at chapel).
- Line 451, "whatever my friends urged". Brewster-0171 says "many persons of distinction, and particularly the Abbé Conti". This is a fair paraphrase.
- Line 501/507 (Einstein). The guest does not adopt relativity. He answers from his Rule IV and the Opticks on induction.
- Lines 511 and 519 (private reckonings).
  - Newton refers to his own unpublished sheets without saying what they contain. They are his own life's papers, so this is not an anachronism.
  - Rule 7 is respected: the gap is stated, not filled.
  - Disowning them "as prophecy" fits daniel-0179, and fits the 2060 manuscript itself, which says it is written "not to assert when the time of the end shall be".
  - "In what I prepared for the press I set none" is not stated in daniel-0179, but it is consistent with it. It is accepted as the guest's framing of his published Observations.
- Line 603, "Once I stopped a friend from saving me". Now that the line says Newton made the request himself, the framing is fair.
